# LeafGreen target-selection decision

The reconstruction target is the Japanese origin retail release, product code `BPGJ`, software revision 0. Its 16 MiB size, hashes, and header are recorded in `config/target.json`.

English `BPGE` revisions 0 and 1 were also observed; no Japanese revision 1 was present locally. The selected target remains `identified`, not `verified`, because local integrity checks are not independent provenance corroboration or a reproducible matching build. No ROM bytes are stored.
