# LeafGreen Div BIOS stub variant reconstruction

`bpg-j-rev0-retail` is the Japanese origin reference and lacks `Div`. The wrapper is independently reconstructed in 6 of 7 cataloged builds: all English, German, French, Italian, and Spanish releases, at -4 from the verified `LZ77UnCompVram` anchor. The committed source applies only to those builds. Presence and absence are retained without ROM bytes.
